02 / Research

My research spans three interconnected themes:

  • Multimodal AI: Pre-training and post-training for large-scale multimodal models, such as point cloud LLM, vision-language model, and VAE.
  • Agentic AI: Building and harnessing LLM based agentic AI systems, such as orchestration, safety, agentic RL and multi-turn trajectory synthesis.
  • AI for Life Science: Developing AI-driven applications for biomedicine and healthcare, such as drug discovery for aging and radiological diagnosis
